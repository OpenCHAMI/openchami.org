SC26 is the premier international conference for high performance computing, networking, storage, and analysis. OpenCHAMI will be participating in the Birds of a Feather (BoF) sessions with a community-focused discussion about modern HPC system management.

## OpenCHAMI Participation

### OpenCHAMI BoF
**Session**: OpenCHAMI Community Birds of a Feather  
**Time**: Tuesday, November 17, 2026, 12:15pm-1:15pm CST  
**Location**: McCormick Place, Chicago, IL, room W184bcd

OpenCHAMI is an HPSF emerging project and an operator-led open-source control plane that brings cloud-like APIs and modular services to HPC system management. This BoF will help newcomers understand what OpenCHAMI enables today, give current users a forum to compare deployments and priorities, and invite operators, vendors, and developers to shape the roadmap.

The discussion will cover adoption paths, including but not limited to xCAT transitions, and will collect community feedback on tooling, integration, documentation, and operational requirements.

**Topic areas**: System Software; Emerging Hardware and Software Technologies  
**Keywords**: System Management, OpenCHAMI, xCAT alternative

#### Session leaders

- **Alex Lovell-Troy**, Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) — OpenCHAMI founder and Technical Steering Committee chair.
- **Jay DePasse**, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) — OpenCHAMI contributor and Technical Steering Committee member managing HPE's OpenCHAMI development effort.
- **John Lockman**, Dell — OpenCHAMI contributor and Technical Steering Committee member leading OpenCHAMI integration into Dell's Omnia project.
- **Sadaf Alam**, University of Bristol — Director of Advanced Computing Strategy and CTO at the Bristol Centre for Supercomputing.
